# Serenity layout — research and implementation notes

The ship faces left in the game. Port is at the bottom of the plan and starboard is at the top when facing the bow. This is a show-oriented interpretation informed by the references below, not a reconstruction verified against an official measured blueprint.

## Connections incorporated

- **Upper level, fore to aft:** bridge → neck corridor → galley/mess → aft passage → engine room. Four hatches in the neck open to ladder-access crew quarters: Mal and Kaylee on starboard; Jayne and Zoe/Wash on port. The interiors are separate simplified playable rooms.
- **Forward vertical connection:** the neck corridor leads to a raised cargo-bay catwalk; stairs connect that level to the cargo floor. The playable catwalk links to the port shuttle and Inara's starboard shuttle. Shuttles can be entered but cannot be flown separately.
- **Lower level, fore to aft:** cargo airlock and cargo bay → common area and infirmary → passenger accommodations. The aft stairs connect the common area with the upper aft passage. The passenger space is a simplified separate playable view with Simon, River and Book stations, not a verified room-by-room recreation.
- **Escape pods:** a forward pod appears on the exterior plan near the bridge. Available sources support shuttle access from the catwalk, but do not establish separate escape pods there, so no catwalk pods have been asserted. The bridge-area safety station cannot launch.

## Known gaps

The active upper- and lower-deck floors and outlines are drawn from the same simplified shapes used for walkability; older AI-generated illustrations are subdued behind them. They are an interpretation and not a measured reproduction. The gameplay layout uses simplified transition views rather than a continuous three-dimensional cutaway; the dimensions, precise hatch order, room sizes, catwalk height, and details of the cabin interiors are not verified. Most furniture is decorative rather than collision-enabled; the dining table is an exception. The background includes speculative furniture and architectural details. The show and film also changed some sets, especially the cargo bay and forward door. An exact reconstruction would need an authoritative set of licensed plans and/or careful shot-by-shot set documentation, and art and collision maps authored together from those measurements.
